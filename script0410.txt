OBJECTIVE:
Introduce the Factor Theorem as a special case of the Remainder Theorem.

TEACHER SCRIPT:
"If you divide 12 by 4, you get exactly 3, with zero remainder. That means 4 is a perfect factor of 12. The exact same logic applies to algebra. This is the Factor Theorem."

ASK THE STUDENT:
"In your own words, what does the Factor Theorem tell us?"

EXPECTED RESPONSE:
"If you plug a number in and get zero, then it's a factor."

IF CORRECT:
"Exactly. It's the ultimate test for finding factors."

WHAT TO EMPHASIZE:
A remainder of zero is the only thing that confirms a factor.
